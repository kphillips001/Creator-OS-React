ARCHITECTURE REBASE

PURPOSE

The migration roadmap was originally built around the assumption that Fanvue remained the primary identity platform.

That assumption is no longer valid.

The target architecture has changed.

Telegram will become the primary communication channel and primary user identity.

Fanvue will be used only as a payment processor and potentially a media-link provider.

KVIQA may become the primary CRM and vault.

Future payment providers may include:

- Fanvue
- DropFans
- Other checkout providers

The architecture must avoid dependency on any specific payment platform.

--------------------------------------------------
OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------

Re-evaluate the migration architecture using:

Telegram = Primary Identity

Payment Platforms = Replaceable Commerce Layer
