# Explicit canonical planner

The Explicit Content canonical planner uses seven ordered, immutable layers:

1. **Scene** owns environment, premise, composition, camera framing and angle,
   lighting, activity, visibility, and progression.
2. **Explicit Editorial Guidance** owns the Explicit workflow's artistic
   language: private premium creator editorial imagery, intimate atmosphere,
   intentional posing, cinematic intimacy, quiet confidence, emotional
   tension, magnetic presence, and sophisticated luxury finish.
3. **Editorial Direction** owns emotional tone, facial expression, eyes and eye
   contact, body language, editorial energy, visual storytelling, subject
   awareness, and camera engagement.
4. **Wardrobe** owns clothing and state of undress.
5. **Creator Identity** owns reference face, body, hair, skin tone, and
   proportions.
6. **Visual Quality** owns cinematography, environmental realism, skin detail,
   anatomy realism, and image-quality language.
7. **Provider Optimization** owns provider render locks and request-specific
   technical guidance.

Each layer may clarify or enrich only its own responsibility. It must not
rewrite, replace, soften, or reinterpret another layer. In particular,
provider optimization cannot assign a generic smile or influencer expression
when Editorial Direction specifies another emotional performance.

Editorial Direction is derived from each selected concept by the planning
model. It is stored as structured internal metadata and rendered into Prompt
Preview between Scene and Creator Identity. It is not shown as a separate
operator control.

## Social and Explicit guidance are intentionally separate

Social Editorial Guidance supports Social Content, X, Instagram, lifestyle,
manual premium concepts, and autonomous inspiration. It favors observed
moments, day-in-the-life movement, environmental interaction, candid creator
energy, and scene-aware fashion styling.

Explicit Editorial Guidance is exclusive to Explicit Inspire, Create From
Tags, the canonical Explicit planner, and future Explicit workflows. It favors
private premium creator editorial imagery, intentional sensual body language,
cinematic intimacy, quiet confidence, magnetic presence, emotional tension,
and sophisticated luxury styling.

The two systems do not share an artistic prior. Explicit planning does not
import Social Editorial Guidance, and Social workflows retain their existing
guidance. Neither guidance system owns Scene, Editorial Direction, Wardrobe,
Creator Identity, Visual Quality, or Provider Optimization, so it cannot
rewrite those layers.
