# Creator Intelligence Center Polish

## Homepage philosophy

Creator Intelligence Center is the operational homepage of Creator_OS. It reports
the current operating mode, recent observed activity, system health, noteworthy
facts, and navigation into the workflows that own operational changes.

The page is deliberately descriptive. It does not coach the creator, prescribe
goals, or introduce a second recommendation system.

## Current Focus

`Current Focus` replaces the former goal-oriented language. It is derived from
the active Commerce Mode and runtime status:

- `Relationship Building` describes Relationship Mode.
- `Commerce Live` describes enabled commerce.
- `Maintenance` describes Commerce OFF or an offline runtime.

Commerce Mode remains editable from the hero through the existing Operations
module-switch API. Business Operations remains the read-only secondary view.

## Relationship Pulse

Relationship Pulse groups observed relationship and conversation facts already
available in the Creator Intelligence response. Missing measurements are shown
as `Untracked`; the frontend does not estimate them.

## Ava Insights

Ava Insights contains factual observations assembled from existing counters and
labels, such as returning visitors, recorded content demand, and Would Have Sold
events. Existing recommendation copy and actions are intentionally excluded.

Opportunities and Needs Attention follow the same evidence-only boundary.
System Health remains a compact status summary, while detailed operation remains
owned by Operations and provider administration.

## Ava Coach boundary

Coaching, suggested actions, opinions, and improvement recommendations are
reserved for the future Ava Coach module. Navigation contains a standard
placeholder labelled `Coming after Version 1 launch`; no Ava Coach backend or
special route was introduced.
